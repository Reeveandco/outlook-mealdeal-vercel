Privacy: The Outlook at Fox's app and order pages

This covers The Outlook at Fox's app for iPhone and Android and the order pages it links to. The Outlook is the café at Fox's Marina, The Strand, Ipswich IP2 8NJ, run by My Gathering Ltd, Stonham Road, Mickfield, Stowmarket, Suffolk IP14 5LS. My Gathering Ltd is responsible for the information described here.

What we collect

When you order we collect your name, email address, mobile number (optional for office orders), and the details of each order: what you ordered, the collection day and time, and the price. If you tick the box for news and offers, we record that too.

The app keeps your contact details, your last order and, for Fox's staff, the staff code on your phone so you don't have to type them each time. Signing out removes them from the phone.

Card payments

Card payments are taken by Square. Your card number is entered into Square's own payment form and goes straight to Square; we never see or store it. Square's privacy notice is at squareup.com/gb/en/legal/general/privacy.

What we use it for

We use your details to make up your order, have it ready at the time you chose, send your confirmation email and contact you if there's a problem with the order. If you opted in, we may also email you about The Outlook. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone other than Square and the email service that sends order confirmations.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as we need them for our accounts, which is normally six years. If you opted in to news and offers, we keep your email for that until you ask us to stop.

Your choices

You can stop marketing emails at any time by emailing us. You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing theoutlookatfoxs@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

No tracking

The app does not use advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking.
